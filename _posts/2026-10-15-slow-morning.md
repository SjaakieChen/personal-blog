---
title: "A slower morning"
date: 2026-10-15 11:00:00 +0100
quote: "Leave a little space before the day begins."
sample: true
---

<p>There is a particular quiet before the first task of the day. The kettle clicks off. Light reaches the edge of the desk. For a moment, nothing has to happen faster than it happens.</p>

<p>It is tempting to fill that space immediately. But a few minutes without a screen can change the pace of the morning. The work is still there afterwards; it just feels less like a race to catch up.</p>
