---
title: "A few things worth keeping"
date: 2026-10-15 12:00:00 +0100
quote: "Not everything useful needs to become something."
sample: true
---

<p>A notebook is a good place for thoughts that have not yet decided what they are. A sentence overheard on a train, a question from a lecture, a description of the sky: none of these needs an explanation before it earns a place on the page.</p>

<p>The interesting part often comes later. Two unrelated notes turn out to share an idea. A question that seemed small opens into something larger. Keeping a record is less about remembering everything than leaving a few doors open.</p>
