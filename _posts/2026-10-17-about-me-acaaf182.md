<p>Hello,</p>
<p>I am Jiaqi Chen, and if you pronounce the &quot;q&quot; in Jiaqi as a &quot;k&quot; then you get my Dutch/English name, a name that I share with a very famous actor. I was born and raised in Belgium. From age 3 to 15, I lived in the towns surrounding Gent, like Heusden or Merelbeke, changing a few times as my parents found various different jobs and homes. I would spend most summers in China, meeting family there, as we were the only ones in our family not in living in China (a fact my grandpa is very proud of).</p>
<p>I have read some biographies before; from the Walter Isaacson ones about Jobs<sup class="citation" contenteditable="false" data-reference="isaacson-jobs-2011" data-locator=""><a href="#ref-acaaf182-d959-4b30-9df9-aa13befc37d1-2" aria-label="Reference 2" title="">[2]</a></sup> and Musk<sup class="citation" contenteditable="false" data-reference="isaacson-musk-2023" data-locator=""><a href="#ref-acaaf182-d959-4b30-9df9-aa13befc37d1-3" aria-label="Reference 3" title="">[3]</a></sup>, to more niche ones about Henry Kaiser<sup class="citation" contenteditable="false" data-reference="foster-kaiser-1989" data-locator=""><a href="#ref-acaaf182-d959-4b30-9df9-aa13befc37d1-4" aria-label="Reference 4" title="">[4]</a></sup> or Pauli<sup class="citation" contenteditable="false" data-reference="jung-pauli-letters" data-locator=""><a href="#ref-acaaf182-d959-4b30-9df9-aa13befc37d1-5" aria-label="Reference 5" title="">[5]</a></sup>. But those are about people who have done remarkable things and the book about justifying their upbringing in relation to their life&#39;s work. I fortunately am not that old and accomplished yet, and so my essay will be a bit different. The 2 parts of the essay will be about; upbringing and aspirations.</p>
<p><strong>Upbringing</strong><strong><br></strong>So, I grew up here in Belgium, going through the normal curriculum. I was very fond of my primary school year&#39;s; I thought I fit right in and had good friends. Aside from that I was also good in school stuff like math, whether that was because of talent or the Asian parenting style is up for debate. Evidence for that could be that I was top 100 in the &quot;Kangoeroewedstrijd&quot;<sup class="citation" contenteditable="false" data-reference="kangoeroe-competition" data-locator=""><a href="#ref-acaaf182-d959-4b30-9df9-aa13befc37d1-6" aria-label="Reference 6" title="">[6]</a></sup> then. But the important thing was that my principle and parents ended up deciding for me to skip grade 4, and I had to adjust to that. I ended up changing schools near the end of year 5 because the commute was too long after us moving homes, and when I graduated from my second primary school, I ended up doing Latin for 3 years at Don-Bosco, before moving to China for High-school. Now I am studying physics at Imperial College in London. The frequent moving has had a large and positive impact on me, though it’s always hard to prove the counterfactual.</p>
<p>Aside from doing all the things that people do, like playing football or video games, I also did spend a lot of effort on chess. The Belgian chess scene is really wonderful; my coaches would take us by car or train to various tournaments organized by the federation. Every first weekend of the month a &quot;criteriumtoernooi&quot; would be organized with hundreds of people per age category. For essentially free I would participate and compete and see my friends there. I ended up being a decent player, being Flemish champion a few times and representing Belgium twice at the world cup equivalent for youth chess.<sup class="citation explanation-marker" contenteditable="false" data-note="dardha-cohort-note"><a href="#ref-acaaf182-d959-4b30-9df9-aa13befc37d1-note-1" aria-label="Explanation 1">*</a></sup></p>
<p><strong>Aspirations</strong><strong><br></strong>Like any person I have wondered about the big questions like: &quot;Who are you?&quot; or &quot;What should you do?&quot;. My answers to them keep iterating, and right now these two questions have merged for me. Who someone is cannot be superficially answered, it&#39;s not a name because people change their names, it&#39;s not an appearance because those can change too. A ship of Theseus is not defined by its planks and sail but by something else. For <strong>people</strong> it seems to me, are defined by their preferences. In big ways like what they see as good or bad, and also in smaller ways like would they rather read a book or play a sport. What those preferences are for me, is hard to collapse down into a few sentences, but just to try; I don&#39;t like seeing misallocation. I like seeing people flourish, a gardener or chef showing their craft is always incredible to see.</p>
<p>I do well when I have<em> interesting</em> people to talk to and<em> interesting</em> things to pursue. And for me the physics department at Imperial has been really great in providing both. Now my immediate goal is to find that next place for me. And long-term, I hope to find my generation&#39;s equivalent for a Bell Labs or Xerox Park. The reason I stopped playing chess when moving to China was because of the opposite of what I described here. There, I was told to be more robotic, memorize more lines, take less risks.</p>
