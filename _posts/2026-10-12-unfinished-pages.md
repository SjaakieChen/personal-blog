---
title: "In praise of unfinished pages"
date: 2026-10-12 08:00:00 +0100
quote: "A beginning is allowed to remain a beginning."
sample: true
---

<p>An unfinished page can feel like evidence of failure. It can also be evidence that an idea was worth trying. Not every sketch needs to become a drawing, and not every paragraph needs to become an essay.</p>

<p>The useful habit is returning. Read the old lines, keep the ones that still feel alive, and let the others rest. There is no requirement to finish every thought in the order it arrived.</p>
