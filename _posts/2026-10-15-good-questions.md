---
title: "The shape of a good question"
date: 2026-10-15 10:00:00 +0100
quote: "Understanding often begins with a better question."
sample: true
---

<p>A difficult idea can look like a wall when the question is too large. Why does this work? is sometimes less useful than What changes if this one assumption disappears? The smaller question gives the mind something it can hold.</p>

<p>That is one pleasure of learning: discovering that confusion has a shape. Once its edges become visible, it stops being a vague feeling and becomes something to investigate.</p>
