---
title: "Room for less"
date: 2026-10-12 07:00:00 +0100
quote: "Clarity is often a matter of leaving something out."
sample: true
---

<p>A page becomes easier to read when its parts stop competing for attention. A title, a date, a few paragraphs, enough space between them. The structure can be quiet and still do its job.</p>

<p>The same idea works beyond a page. Fewer open tabs, fewer things on a desk, fewer commitments made out of habit. Less is not always better, but it can make the important things easier to see.</p>
