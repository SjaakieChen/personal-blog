---
title: "Taking the long way home"
date: 2026-10-12 09:00:00 +0100
quote: "A familiar place can still surprise you."
sample: true
---

<p>The shortest route is not always the most memorable one. A different turning reveals a narrow garden, a window full of books, or a tree that has changed colour since last week. These are small discoveries, but they make a familiar place feel larger.</p>

<p>A walk does not need to solve anything. Sometimes it is enough to return with colder hands and a little more room in your thoughts.</p>
