Photonic alignment at imec

At imec’s CMST group at Ghent University, I modelled the optics and developed a procedure for positioning movable ball lenses to couple laser light into a chip waveguide.

Regenerative neural cellular automaton

I reproduced the Growing Neural Cellular Automata method in PyTorch using a Noto Emoji image as the target. I set up resumable training on a repurposed laptop and tested how the trained model grows and repairs the pattern.

Shadow CT scanner

My physics group at Imperial College London built a scanner that reconstructs objects from visible-light shadows. I worked on the reconstruction software and on getting the camera, turntable and electronics to work together. I set up the TIGRE reconstruction library on an NVIDIA GPU.

Modular wooden tiles

I designed a flat wooden tile with slots and notches that could be laser cut and assembled into three-dimensional structures. I used friction to hold the joints together and designed modules that could rotate.

Fibre optics research

For my IB Physics extended essay, I used a second fibre on an alignment bench to sample the optical power from a single-mode fibre at different positions. I compared the measurements with a Gaussian beam model.
