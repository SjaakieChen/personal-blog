<p>Year 1 Physics summer group project · Imperial College London · 2025.</p>
<p>Using visible light, a motor-driven turntable and a camera, our group explored how an object&#39;s shape can be reconstructed from shadows recorded at multiple angles. The poster covers automated image acquisition, weighting, filtering and back-projection, and the artefacts seen in reconstructed chess pieces.</p>
<p><strong>Group project: </strong>Tejas Penugonda, Harshul Chand, Jiaqi Chen and Ivan Wong.</p>
