<p>Misalignment of integrated lasers on photonic chips has been a big issue. The group I was working in was trying to solve this issue by intentionally misplacing the laser and having automated movable lenses focus the laser into the waveguide instead. I modelled the optics and designed the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B5ln2s0bnAY">pre-alignment and alignment procedures</a>, which they did not have before. I integrated the machine's custom software and programming language, YASE, with Python. My solution was handed off and is still in use for the ongoing project.</p>
