<p>A shadow captures an object's outline from one direction. Our shadow CT device collects shadows from multiple angles and combines them into a 3D reconstruction. My friends and I built the device, and I worked on the reconstruction software and getting the electronics, hardware and software to work together.</p>

<p>The hardest part was getting the TIGRE reconstruction library to work with my NVIDIA GPU. It took a lot of setup, but this was necessary to run the reconstruction.</p>

<p>Here is a <a href="#media-image/assets/evidence/ct-scanner/knight-shadow-apparatus.png">knight casting a shadow</a> in the scanner. The knight sits on the rotating platform, and the phone records its shadow on the screen.</p>

<p>Here are our <a href="#media-image/assets/evidence/ct-scanner/printed-chess-set.png">3D-printed chess reconstructions</a>. The dark pieces in front are the printed reconstructions; the white pieces behind are the original objects.</p>

<p>The <a href="#media-evidence--ct-scanner-page-1">project poster</a> shows the device and a reconstructed chess set. These results give a sense of what the scanner could do with our limited budget.</p>
