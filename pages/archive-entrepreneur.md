<p>When talking about good entrepreneurship ideas with friends, we settled on designing toys. The idea was that, given limited resources and a limited network, toys are a relatively even playing field because good toys are simple. I ended up designing <a href="https://sjaakiechen.github.io/personal-blog/assets/evidence/entrepreneur/spark-demonstration.mp4">this repeatable wooden tile design</a> that allows for the building of large, beautiful structures relatively simply. I also filed for <a href="https://sjaakiechen.github.io/personal-blog/assets/evidence/entrepreneur/registered-design.pdf">design registration</a> and set up a payment checkout system.</p>
